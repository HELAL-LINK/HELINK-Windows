HELINK: a complete download manager for Windows 11 and 10 - version 2.4.0
=========================================================================
HELINK downloads files and videos on Windows:
- Any file (programs, documents, archives, music, video) downloads faster: HELINK splits it into
  parts that download together over up to 16 connections, and continues where it stopped after
  the internet drops or the computer turns off.
- It takes the downloads you start in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi and
  Firefox) once the HELINK extension is added to the browser.
- It downloads videos from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X and hundreds of other sites, in
  the quality you choose up to 4K or as MP3 audio, with the original (not dubbed) sound.
- It puts a Download button on videos in the browser; you can drag the button anywhere.
- It removes the music from videos and audio and keeps speech and singing: while downloading, or
  for files on your computer.
- It downloads a whole YouTube course or playlist at once, numbered in order in one folder.
- It downloads just a part of any video: play it, set where it starts and ends, then save it as
  video or MP3.
- It downloads the photos and videos of products on Amazon, Alibaba, AliExpress, Noon and Temu at
  full size.
- It tells you by itself when a new version is out, and updates itself in one click when you say so.
- In Arabic and English, no ads, no accounts, no sign-in.

New in version 2.4:
- A part of a video: click "Part of a video" at the top of the window and paste the link, or click
  "A part only" in the "Download a video" window. The video plays in the window: set its start and
  end on the timeline, play the part to check it, then download it as video or MP3. (Section 6)
- Product photos and videos: paste a product's link from Amazon, Alibaba, AliExpress, Noon or Temu,
  or click the HELINK button in your browser on the product's page. All its photos and videos
  appear; pick the ones you want and they're saved at full size in a folder named after the
  product. (Section 7)
- Browser downloads, more exactly: the browser's download bar no longer shows a file HELINK takes,
  and a "HELINK is downloading this file" card appears in the page, so it's clear HELINK has it.
  HELINK also takes more kinds of files.

New in version 2.3:
- A whole course or playlist at once: paste the list's YouTube link and all its videos appear.
  Choose the quality once for all of them (or MP3 audio) and tick what you want: they are saved in
  one folder named after the list, numbered in its order (01, 02, ...). When you paste the same list
  later, HELINK ticks only the new lessons. (Section 5)
- Updates from inside HELINK: when you're online, HELINK looks for a new version and shows what's
  new in it. Click "Update now" and it updates itself in seconds, downloads continuing where they
  stopped; or "Not now" and it reminds you a day later. HELINK never updates itself without your
  say-so. (Section 12)


1) Installing
-------------
Recommended: open HELINK-2.4.0-Setup.exe and click Next, then Install.
- It needs no administrator rights, installs for your account only, and adds shortcuts on the
  desktop and in the Start menu.
- If HELINK is running, the installer closes it first; its downloads continue when it opens again.
- Updates: from version 2.3 on, HELINK tells you when a new version is out and updates itself
  (section 12). From an older version: install this one over it; downloads and settings stay.
  Updating from 2.1 or older: close the browser and open it again once, so the new extension (the
  menu of qualities) runs. From 2.2 on, the extension in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and Vivaldi
  updates itself after HELINK is updated.

Or the portable copy: extract HELINK-2.4.0-portable.zip and open HELINK.exe from any folder.
It updates itself too, when it is in a folder Windows lets it change (Downloads or Documents, say).

If a blue screen says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway". Windows
shows this for any new program that is not widespread yet; it won't show again.

The first time it runs:
- HELINK sets up its video tools once (about 146 MB), with the progress at the top of the window.
  You can download ordinary files right away; the tools are needed for videos only.
- The window "Add HELINK to your browser" opens: follow its steps (section 2).
- HELINK starts with Windows, quietly near the clock, to take downloads from the browser at any
  time. You can turn this off in Settings.


2) Adding HELINK to your browser (the most important step)
----------------------------------------------------------
With the HELINK extension:
- HELINK takes the files you start downloading in the browser and downloads them faster.
- A Download button appears when the mouse is over any video; you choose the quality right there.
- The right-click menu gets "Download link with HELINK" on links and "Download this video with
  HELINK" on videos.
- The HELINK button in the browser's toolbar lists the videos and sounds the page played.

Click the extension button (the puzzle piece) at the top of HELINK's window, or "Add to browser" on
the side. The window lists the browsers on your computer and whether the extension is connected.

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and Vivaldi:
  1) Click "Open extensions page" next to your browser (or type chrome://extensions in Chrome,
     edge://extensions in Edge, brave://extensions in Brave).
  2) Turn on "Developer mode" (at the top or the side of the page).
  3) Click "Load unpacked" and choose the folder HELINK's window shows (click "Copy path", then
     paste it in the folder window):
     %LOCALAPPDATA%\HELINK\extension\chromium
  4) Close the browser and open it again. HELINK's window then says "The extension is connected".
  Note: HELINK updates the extension's files in that folder with every new version; don't delete it.
  Tip: pin the HELINK button to the toolbar (the browser's puzzle piece, then the pin next to HELINK).

Firefox:
  - Click "Install in Firefox" in HELINK's window, then "Add". Firefox accepts only add-ons signed
    by Mozilla: if it refuses, this copy is not signed yet.
  - To try it before signing: open about:debugging, then "This Firefox", then "Load Temporary
    Add-on", and choose helink-firefox.xpi ("Show the file" in HELINK's window). It stays until
    Firefox closes.

The extension's settings: the HELINK toolbar button, then Settings; or Settings → Browser in
HELINK itself. Choose the extension's language, the kinds of files it takes, and the sites it leaves
to the browser.


3) Downloading files
--------------------
From the browser: start the download as usual (click the site's download link). HELINK takes it and
opens the "New download" window in front of you:
  - File name: you can change it.
  - Kind: Video, Music, Pictures, Programs, Documents, Compressed, Other (HELINK picks it).
  - Save in: the kind's folder in the save folder, and "Change" to pick another folder for this
    file only.
  - "Start download", or "Later" (added to the list, paused, to start when you like), or "Cancel".
  When HELINK takes several files at once, "1 of 3" shows at the top and they come one by one.
  If you don't want this window, turn off 'Show the "New download" window first' in Settings →
  Browser: downloads then start right away.
  HELINK leaves to the browser: pages and pictures, files smaller than the size you set, the sites
  in "Sites the browser downloads from itself", and downloads it cannot finish by itself.

Where do files go? In the HELINK folder inside Downloads, with a folder for each kind: Video,
  Music, Pictures, Programs, Documents, Compressed, and Other for the rest. Each folder is made with
  the first file of its kind. You can change the save folder, or turn off "A folder for each kind of
  file" to keep everything in the save folder itself, in Settings → Downloads.

By link: click "Add link" (or Ctrl+N), paste the link and click Continue. Or press Ctrl+V in
HELINK's window, or drag the link from the browser into the window. HELINK checks the link: a file
opens the "New download" window; a page with a video shows the video's qualities.

The link expired? Some sites give download links that last a short time. If a download stops with
"The download link expired", start downloading the same file again from the site in your browser:
HELINK knows it is the same file and offers "Continue it with this link", so it continues where it
stopped instead of starting over.


4) Downloading videos
---------------------
From the browser:
  - Move the mouse over the video and click the orange Download button: a small menu opens in the
    page with "Video" and its qualities and "MP3 audio" (the best pick marked "Recommended"), and
    the "Without music" switch (turn it on before choosing, if you want it). Choose a quality and
    HELINK downloads it at once while you stay in the browser; a small window near the clock shows
    how it goes: progress, speed and time left, Pause and Resume, then "Open file" and "Show in
    folder" when it's done. It goes away by itself a little after the downloads finish (one that
    failed stays, with "Try again"); click a download in it to see it in HELINK's window. When
    HELINK's window is open, the download simply appears in its list.
    "Open in HELINK" at the bottom of the menu shows the video in HELINK's window, as before.
    You can drag the button anywhere on the video; HELINK remembers where, for each site.
  - Or right-click the video, then "Download this video with HELINK" (or right-click anywhere on
    the page, then "Download this page's video with HELINK").
  - Or click the HELINK button in the toolbar: it lists the videos the page played.
By link: copy the video's link and paste it in HELINK (Ctrl+V or "Add link").

The "Download a video" window shows the video's picture and title. Choose "Video" or "MP3 audio",
choose the quality (the best pick is marked) and click "Download video". When a post has several
videos (X and Instagram posts), numbered pictures let you pick one. HELINK downloads the video's
original sound, not a dubbed one.
When HELINK finds no video in a page but the page played video or sound files, it offers those.


5) A whole course or playlist
----------------------------
HELINK downloads all the videos of a YouTube playlist at once: a course, a series of lessons, an
album.
  1) Copy the list's link and paste it in HELINK (Ctrl+V or "Add link"). It looks like:
       https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=...
     Or open any video of the list: the "Download a video" window says "This video is part of a
     playlist", with "Download the whole list". In the browser, the same button is at the bottom
     of the Download button's menu on the video.
  2) The "Download a course or playlist" window shows the list's name, how many videos it has and
     how long they are in all, then all its videos in order.
  3) Choose "Video" or "MP3 audio", then the quality, once for every video: "Up to 1080p", "Up to
     720p" (the best pick), "Up to 480p" or "Up to 360p"; for audio 320k, 192k or 128k. A video's
     quality is a ceiling: a video that doesn't have it comes in the best quality below it. You can
     turn on "Without music" as for one video.
  4) All the videos are ticked: untick what you don't want, or use "Select all". The number ticked
     and about how big they are show above the list; then click "Download".
The videos are saved in one folder named after the list, inside Video (or Music for audio), each
name starting with its place in the list, so the lessons stay in order:
       Video\Graphic design for beginners\01 - Welcome.mp4
       Video\Graphic design for beginners\02 - Installing the tools.mp4
- They all go to the downloads list and download like any other: 3 at a time (or as many as you set
  in Settings), the others waiting their turn; pause and resume them as you like. Each one's
  details show the list it came from.
- When you paste the same list later (when new lessons were added to it, say), what you downloaded
  before is marked "In downloads" and only what is new is ticked, so no lesson downloads twice.
- Private, removed and members-only videos show as "Not available" and can't be ticked.
- The window shows the first 1000 videos of a list.
- A whole channel isn't downloaded at once: open the channel's "Playlists", then the list you want,
  and copy its link.


6) A part of a video
--------------------
HELINK downloads just the part you want of any video (a bit of a lecture, part of a song), as video
or MP3:
  1) Click "Part of a video" at the top of the window, paste the video's link and click Continue.
     Or open the video as usual and click "A part only" in the "Download a video" window.
  2) The video plays in the window. Set the part in any of these ways:
     - Drag the two orange handles on the timeline: the first for the start, the second for the
       end; the picture follows them.
     - Or click − and + under "Start" and "End" to move each by a second (hold to keep going).
     - Or play the video and click "Start here" at the moment you want, then "End here".
  3) Click "Play the part" to watch it from its start; it stops by itself at its end.
  4) Choose "Video" or "MP3 audio" and the quality (the part's approximate size is shown), and turn
     on "Without music" if you like.
  5) Click "Download the part". It appears in the downloads list with its times (like
     ✂ 1:20 – 2:05) and is saved under the video's name with the part's times:
     "Title - part 1.20-2.05 - 720p.mp4".
- The part is cut exactly at the second you chose, and little more than the part is downloaded.
- If the video doesn't play inside the window, you can still set the start and end with the
  buttons, and the part is downloaded all the same.


7) Product photos and videos (for traders)
-----------------------------------------
HELINK downloads a product's photos and videos at full size from Amazon (Egypt, the UAE, Saudi
Arabia and the others), Alibaba, AliExpress, Noon and Temu, into one folder named after the
product, to study the product or use its photos.
From the browser (the surest, since it reads the page as you see it):
  - Open the product's page, click the HELINK button in the browser's toolbar and choose
    "Product photos and videos".
  - Or right-click the page and choose "Download the product's photos with HELINK".
By link: click "Product photos" at the top of HELINK's window and paste the product's link, or
paste it in "Add link". Some sites (Temu especially, and sometimes Amazon and AliExpress) don't
let programs read their pages; the window then tells you to use the HELINK button in the browser.
The "A product's photos and videos" window shows the product's name and site, then all its videos
and photos, all chosen. Click one to leave it out (or "Select all"), then click "Download
selected". They're saved in:
       Pictures\<the product's name>\01.jpg, 02.jpg, ... and "video 1.mp4"
- The photos are at full size, not the small ones the page shows, including the photos of every
  colour or model when there are some.
- Amazon's videos are downloaded at the best quality there is.


8) Removing the music
---------------------
HELINK removes the music from a video or a recording and keeps the voices (speech and singing):
useful for songs (only the singer's voice stays) and for lessons, lectures and programs with music
in the background.

When downloading a video: in the "Download a video" window, turn on "Without music" next to
"Video / MP3 audio", then click "Download video without music" (or "Download audio without music").
HELINK downloads the video first, then removes the music, and saves only the copy without music,
its name ending in "(no music)". The video's picture stays exactly as it was.
The switch stays on for the next videos until you turn it off or close HELINK.

Files on your computer (audio or video):
  1) Click "Remove music" at the top of the window (the crossed-out note), or "Choose files on this
     computer" in the "Music removal" section on the side, or "Remove the music from files…" in the
     menu of HELINK's icon near the clock.
  2) Choose one file or more (hold Ctrl to choose several), then click "Remove the music".
  3) The original stays as it is; a new copy is saved next to it, in the same folder, for example:
       lesson.mp4   →   lesson (no music).mp4
     When that folder can't be written to (a CD, say), the copy goes where downloads are saved.
A file in the downloads list: right-click it, then "Remove the music": a copy without music is
saved next to it.

- The first time only, HELINK downloads its music remover (about 64 MB); the list shows "Setting up…".
- The music is removed from one file at a time; the others wait their turn ("Waiting its turn…").
  Downloads are not held up by it.
- On a recent computer it takes about a minute for a 4-minute song, longer on older ones (the list
  shows the time left). It runs at low priority, so the computer stays quick for everything else.
- HELINK keeps the file's format: an MP3 stays MP3 with its cover and tags, an MP4 stays MP4 with
  its subtitles and chapters, and so do M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG, MKV, WEBM and AVI. Rare formats are
  saved as MP3 (audio) or MKV (video).
- Pause, resume and delete work as for any download; resuming starts the removal over (a video that
  was downloaded is not downloaded again).
- A "No music" tag shows next to the name of each file without music; all of them are under
  "Without music" on the side.
- The result is usually very good but not always perfect: a faint trace of music may remain in some
  parts, and the sound may be slightly less clear than the original.


9) The downloads list
---------------------
- Sections on the side: All downloads, Unfinished, Finished, then by kind (Video, Music, Pictures,
  Programs, Documents, Compressed, Other), then "Music removal" (everything without music, and a button to
  choose files on the computer), with how many each has.
- Columns: name, size, status and progress, speed, time left, when it was added. Click a column's
  title to sort by it; type in "Search downloads" to find a name or a site.
- Buttons at the top: Resume, Pause, Delete (for the selected downloads), Pause all, Resume all.
- Click a download to select it; Ctrl or Shift select several. Double-click a finished file to open
  it, or an unfinished download to see its details.
- Right-click a download: Open file, Show in folder, Resume, Pause, Remove the music (a finished
  audio or video file), Copy address, Open the page, Details, Remove from list, Delete with the file.
- Details: the address, folder, size, how much is downloaded, speed, connections, whether it can
  resume, and the "Parts" bar showing what each connection has downloaded.
- Deleting asks first. Unfinished downloads are deleted with what was downloaded; finished files
  stay where they are unless you choose "Also delete the finished files" (to the Recycle Bin).
- HELINK downloads 3 files at once (you can change it); the others wait for their turn.
- If HELINK closes, the computer turns off or the internet drops, downloads pause and stay in the
  list: click Resume or Resume all to continue where they stopped. A few sites don't support
  resuming: the "New download" window says so, and such a download starts over if it stops.
- The computer doesn't sleep while downloading, and the progress shows on HELINK's taskbar button.


10) Near the clock
-----------------
Closing HELINK's window keeps it running near the clock: downloads go on and it still takes
downloads from the browser. Click its icon to open the window; right-click it for a menu: Open
HELINK, Add a link, Remove the music from files, Pause all downloads, Resume all downloads, Exit HELINK;
and "Update HELINK to version ..." when a new version is out.
When HELINK takes a download from the browser while its window is closed, a small "New download"
window appears in front of the browser; click "Open HELINK" at its top to see the whole window.
Downloads whose quality you choose in the menu of the browser's Download button show in a small
window near the clock instead, which leaves the keyboard to the browser (section 4).
A notification says when a download finishes or fails (can be turned off in Settings).


11) Settings
-----------
General: language, start HELINK with Windows, keep it near the clock when the window closes,
  notifications.
Downloads: the save folder (Downloads › HELINK at first), a folder for each kind of file (Video,
  Music, Pictures, Programs, Documents, Compressed, Other inside the save folder; on at first),
  downloads at the same time
  (1 to 10), connections per file (1 to 16; more is usually faster, some sites accept only a few).
Browser: take downloads from the browser, show the "New download" window first, the kinds of files
  it takes, the smallest size it takes, sites the browser downloads from itself, the Download
  button on videos, sites without the button.
Changes are saved right away.


12) Updating HELINK
-------------------
HELINK looks for a new version by itself: a little after it opens, then every 12 hours while it
runs, and a few minutes after the internet comes back when it was down. It sends nothing about you
or your downloads; it only reads a small file from its page on GitHub.
When a new version is out:
  - The window "A new version of HELINK is available" opens: the version, the update's size and
    "What's new". When HELINK's window is closed (near the clock), a notification says so; click it
    to see the update.
  - "Update now": HELINK downloads the update (the window shows how far it is, and "Stop
    downloading"), then closes and opens again in a few seconds, and running downloads continue
    where they stopped. Nothing else to do, and no administrator rights needed.
  - "Not now": your version stays as it is, and HELINK reminds you a day later. The orange "Update"
    button stays at the top of the window to update when you like, and "Update HELINK to version
    ..." is in the menu of its icon near the clock.
  - HELINK never updates itself until you click "Update now".
To check yourself: open About (the i button at the top of the window), then "Check for updates".
Safety: every update is signed, and HELINK checks the signature and the file's fingerprint
(SHA-256) before installing it; a file that was changed, or doesn't match the original, is not
installed.
The portable copy updates itself too when it is in a folder Windows lets it change; otherwise
HELINK asks you to download the new version from its page and install it.
HELINK's download page: https://github.com/HELAL-LINK/HELINK-Windows


13) Keyboard shortcuts
----------------------
Ctrl+N or Ctrl+L: add a link.          Ctrl+V: paste a link and check it.
Ctrl+F: search.                        Ctrl+A: select all downloads.
Delete: delete the selected downloads (Shift+Delete: with the finished files).
Enter: open the finished file, or the download's details.      Arrows: move in the list.
Esc: close the open window or the details.


14) If something goes wrong
---------------------------
- "Try again" usually fixes it: HELINK updates its video tool by itself when a site changes, and
  continues downloads where they stopped.
- The extension doesn't connect? Make sure HELINK is installed, then restart the browser. "Add
  HELINK to your browser" shows the connection; the extension's settings have "Test the connection".
- The browser downloads a file itself? Check that the file's kind is in "Kinds of files it takes"
  and that the site isn't in "Sites the browser downloads from itself". HELINK leaves a few
  downloads to the browser on purpose (forms, payment pages, downloads that need special steps) so
  they don't fail.
- HELINK downloads public videos; videos of private accounts cannot be downloaded.
- Removing music: "Couldn't set up music removal" means the remover's download (64 MB) didn't
  finish: check the internet and click "Try again". "This file has no sound" or "Can't read this
  file" mean the file has no sound, or isn't a sound file or video in good shape. "File not found"
  means the original was moved or deleted.
- The window is empty? HELINK waits a few seconds, then loads it again by itself (no need to end it
  in the Task Manager). If it never shows, a message says so: restart the computer, and if it
  happens again, install Microsoft Edge WebView2 again.
- The update failed? Check the internet and click "Try again" in the update's window: it continues
  where it stopped. If it happens again, click "Download page", download the new version and
  install it over the old one. An update that didn't finish leaves the previous version running.
- A course doesn't show? Make sure the link is to a public or unlisted playlist (private lists
  can't be read), then click "Try again".
- If an antivirus objects to HELINK or its tools, add HELINK and this folder to its exceptions:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\HELINK
- If a download fails, open its details, click "Details for support", then "Copy", and send what
  was copied.
- HELINK needs Microsoft Edge WebView2, which Windows 11 and most Windows 10 computers have. If it
  is missing, HELINK shows a message that opens its download page at Microsoft.


15) Removing HELINK
-------------------
Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → HELINK → Uninstall. It asks whether to delete the
settings, the downloads list and the video tools (with the music remover) too. The files you downloaded stay where they are.
Then remove the extension from the browser (extensions page → HELINK → Remove).
Portable copy: delete HELINK.exe, then delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\HELINK
